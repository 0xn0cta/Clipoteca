# Logo concept — Clipoteca

## Idea

The mark combines a **C-shaped collection frame** with a small **index tab**. The outer contour suggests a clip/frame that can hold a media item; the tab gives it the character of a filed, retrievable collection. It avoids a literal book, play triangle, film reel, or download arrow.

## Lockup

- `clipoteca-logo.svg`: horizontal symbol + lowercase wordmark for documents and brand references.
- `frontend/src/assets/clipoteca-mark.svg`: transparent mark used in the desktop app header.
- `frontend/public/favicon.svg`: same symbol on the app’s blue-ink tile for browser/Windows icon use.

## Color and type

- Muted iris `#B7A6E3` carries the frame and index-tab accent.
- Soft ivory `#F0EEE9` carries the inner item/frame detail and wordmark.
- Wordmark uses the app’s Manrope family and bold weight for consistency with the refreshed interface.

## Usage

- Use the full lockup on dark blue-ink backgrounds; use the mark-only variant in compact app chrome.
- Preserve the clear space around the open right side of the C.
- Keep the mark flat and single-color/duotone; do not add gradients, glows, or extra playback symbols.

This is the first vector direction for review, not a trademark or availability clearance.
